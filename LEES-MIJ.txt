ORBIT PACT
==========

GETTING STARTED
1. Extract the entire download. Keep js and vendor next to index.html.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. Choose a contract, assign Scout-1 and start your first mission.

No installation, account, API key or internet connection is required for
the downloaded game. Opening index.html inside an unextracted zip will
not load the other required files.

CONTROLS
- Drag the globe to rotate. Click a country to filter its contracts.
- Use the country selector next to the map to select a country directly.
- Choose "Find new contracts" to find offers for the selected country.
- Switch between Globe and World map using the buttons above the map.
- Use the mouse wheel or plus/minus buttons to zoom. The centre button resets.
- On touchscreens, drag with one finger and use plus/minus to zoom.
- With the map focused: arrow keys move, + and - zoom, Home resets.
- Use 3x to speed up game time. The pause button stops the simulation.

GAME RULES
Start with EUR 100,000 and one Scout-1. Pay the start cost once when you
accept a contract. Receive its reward on completion.
Each satellite can work on one contract at a time. Its class must meet
the contract requirement. Class 1 offers remain available at higher levels.

MISSION CHOICES AND SPECIALTIES
Quick scans take half the standard time, with smaller rewards and start costs.
Standard missions balance commitment and payout. Long-term contracts unlock
at 5 completions: three times the duration, 3.4 times the reward and twice
the start cost before upgrades. Refresh offers to find newly unlocked work.

Match a satellite specialty to gain +20% reward and 15% less time:
- Relay-1: communications support and maritime monitoring.
- Nimbus-1: weather, environmental research and disaster monitoring.
- Observer-1: mapping data, border mapping and strategic support.
- Advanced-1: every contract category.
Scout-1 remains a generalist. Every satellite may perform non-matching
assignments if its class is sufficient; those assignments get no specialty bonus.
Relay-1 and Nimbus-1 unlock after 3 completed contracts.
The contract briefing lists matching satellites first and shows the exact quote.

OBJECTIVES
Open Objectives to track 7 milestones. Unlocks apply as soon as the goal is
reached. Use Claim reward to collect each cash/research prize once.
Objective cash does not count toward company-level contract revenue.
Older saves are eligible for milestones based on their recorded progress.
Countries served in older versions are reconstructed from the saved history.

Contract profit = reward minus start cost, excluding fleet maintenance.
A game month lasts 30 game minutes. Maintenance is charged according to
elapsed game time. A Scout-1 costs EUR 500 per game month before upgrades.
At 3x speed, contracts, maintenance and wear all advance three times faster.
Pausing or hiding the tab stops the simulation. There is no offline income.
Satellite paths are a game visualization, not a physical orbit simulation.
Coverage increases rewards; contracts have no geographic restriction.

Upgrades affect all current and future satellites:
- Resolution: +10% reward per level.
- Coverage: +5 percentage points of coverage and +3% reward per level.
- Efficiency: -10% maintenance and +4 percentage points of efficiency per level.
  Higher efficiency reduces the duration of new contracts.
- Reliability: wear divided by 1 + 0.4 x level.
- Processing: -8% contract duration per level.
- Communication: -8% start cost per level.
Reward, start cost and duration are fixed when a contract starts.

Completed contracts earn research points. Each upgrade costs money and
research points. Each category has five levels.
Company levels require total contract revenue of EUR 0, 100,000, 500,000,
2,000,000 and 5,000,000. The maximum company level is five.

SAVING
Progress saves locally every 15 seconds and after important actions,
including purchases, upgrades and contract starts or completions.
The game also saves when leaving or hiding the tab.
Use Company to save manually or start a new game.
Storage depends on your browser. Private browsing or blocked local storage
can prevent progress from being retained after closing.
Clearing browser data removes local saves. Another browser, website or
local file location may use separate storage. Check the footer for save status.

Use Company > Export save to download your current progress as JSON.
Use Import save to preview and confirm a replacement game. Imports start
paused and preserve your old game under Restore previous save. Invalid files
do not replace your company. Keep an exported file if you clear browser data.

This release keeps saves from the previous English and Dutch releases,
including money, satellites, active contracts and upgrades. Old activity
messages are translated when loaded. The internal save keys stay unchanged.

Version 1 saves at the same browser location are imported if present:
balance, satellites, reputation, research and total revenue are retained.
Old active assignments are released. Upgrade data and completed-contract
counts that version 1 did not save cannot be recovered.
The original version 1 storage entry is preserved.

CLOUD SAVES
Company > Create cloud save uploads your current company to Supabase.
Keep the private recovery code shown after the first upload. Anyone with
this code can load or replace that online save. The code cannot be recovered
if you lose it and clear your browser data.

Use Save online to update the cloud copy. Cloud uploads are manual.
To continue on another device: open Company > Enter recovery code, paste
your code, review the company and select Load save. Your local company is
kept as a backup and the loaded game starts paused.

If another device has saved a newer revision, load that cloud save before
uploading again. Internet connection problems do not stop local gameplay.
Disconnect this device keeps both the local company and its online copy.
Recovery codes are not included in exported game JSON files. Store the code
separately if you want to reconnect to the same cloud slot.

Run from this folder: node tests/regression.cjs
These checks cover game logic, map projections and save compatibility.
They do not cover visual browser layout. Normal play does not need Node.js.


SPACEPORT EXPANSION (2.3)
Open Spaceport and name your company. Choose land on the map, click a country at the point where you want to settle, then click Found company here. Confirm your name and found the company for free.
Your headquarters and four plots are included. Select an empty owned plot to build a satellite factory (EUR 12,000) and another for a launch pad (EUR 8,000). When construction finishes, order a Scout-1 for EUR 52,500. Once manufacturing finishes, click Launch. The satellite joins your fleet after the countdown.
Headquarters supplies 4 power. A completed solar array adds 8. Build solar before adding extra factories or research campuses. Buy adjoining land to expand. Research campuses earn one research point per game minute. Mission control speeds up future manufacturing orders.
The clock controls all construction and production. Pause or hide the tab to stop time. Existing satellites and contracts remain available. Cloud saves include your entire spaceport when you click Save online under Company.


MULTIPLAYER AND SOLO MATCHES (2.4)
At the start screen, enter your company name and choose a headquarters country.
Multiplayer: Create lobby, share the eight-character code with friends, and have them use Join with code. Choose different countries. Everyone clicks Ready. The host clicks Start match.
Solo: choose Easy, Normal or Hard and click Start solo match. You face three bots with equal starting resources.
Matches have separate progress from free play. Start with EUR 100,000 and one satellite. Accept contracts from the shared market, build your spaceport, and be the first to EUR 250,000 contract revenue. A match lasts at most 30 minutes. Build a factory and pad before launching extra satellites. Solar provides power; a research lab makes contracts faster.
Use Menu to return to the start screen. Resume solo match or Resume online lobby restores your match on this browser. Online time continues for everyone even when a tab is closed. Online lobbies expire after 48 hours. Your free-play company remains available under Continue free play.
